## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**
'Smooth Criminal Slayer' is a Demon Slayer-inspired game where the final villain is a powerful demon known as Michael Jackson.

**Storyline:**

It is nighttime at the Butterfly Mansion, where a powerful demon named Michael Jackson has infiltrated the mansion. The player's goal is to explore the mansion and collect a Flame guard, uniform, fox mask, sword, wisteria, sunlight orb, doctor's antidote, and a bowl of ramen before confronting Michael Jackson. All eight items are required to defeat him. If the player enters Michael Jackson's room before collecting every item, they will be turned into a demon and forced to serve Michael Jackson for eternity.
